TRACE WITH ECHO

Which Echo should it open?

Select a claim on any page and click the echo. Trace button next to it: Echo traces it in the side panel. Or right-click it and choose Trace with Echo to open Echo in a new tab with the claim filled in; nothing is traced there until you press Trace sources.

Your own Echo, for example http://localhost:3000 while running npm run dev, or a deployed address such as https://echo.example. Only the site part is kept. For a deployed address, the browser asks once to let the side panel use your Echo sign-in there.

The pop-up next to a selection

It appears at the end of a selection of 8 characters or more (never in text fields), and goes away when you scroll, click elsewhere or press Escape. Trace runs the selection as a claim; Check links (when the selection has links) checks them.

Shortcut: Alt+Shift+E traces the selected text the same way.

Let Echo start runs from the side panel

Echo starts a run from the side panel only for an extension it lists. Add this line to Echo's .env.local (or the deployment's environment) and restart Echo:

ECHO_EXTENSION_ORIGINS=chrome-extension://…

Until then the side panel cannot show Echo; use the right-click menu, which opens Echo in a tab.

What it can see

  • Only what you select or right-click: the selected text and the links inside it, or the page or link address for Find claims in this page. The pop-up's script reads the selection on the page to place its button; nothing leaves the page until you click it.
  • It sends that text to the Echo address above only: in the address of the Echo tab it opens (at most 500 characters), or to the Echo page in the side panel after you click the pop-up or press the shortcut.
  • It stores two settings, the Echo address and the pop-up switch, in your browser's extension storage, and the last pop-up click for two minutes in session storage.
